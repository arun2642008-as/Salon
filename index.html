Our Story

A salon built on unhurried craft

Lumière opened with one idea: great beauty work takes time, attention, and a room that doesn't rush you. Every appointment is scheduled with space to breathe — no double-booked chairs, no clock-watching.

Our team trains continuously in colour theory, cutting technique, and nail artistry, so whether you're in for a trim or a full transformation, you're in careful hands.
